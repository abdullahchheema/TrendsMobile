Each shop must confirm

  1. Open the shared server URL and sign in with the assigned staff login.
  2. Confirm the correct shop, stock, staff permissions and payment methods.
  3. Scan a real label, choose the correct phone/warehouse and check the price.
  4. Print the test and an approved sample receipt, including PTA status, warranty and Urdu text.
  5. Verify operation while internet is disconnected and the LAN/server stay connected.
  6. Test reconnection with an unsaved cart. Keep uncertain sale results on the same page and review invoices before starting another sale.
