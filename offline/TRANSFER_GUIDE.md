# Stock transfers between shops (owner only)

Only the owner can create, dispatch, receive or cancel a transfer. Shop logins have no transfer screens; they simply see their own
stock change when you complete one. Open **Stock Transfers** in the owner menu.

## The steps
1. **New transfer**: choose the sending shop, the destination shop, and the phones (tick them, or scan or type their IMEIs). Press
   **Save as pending** to prepare it, or **Create and dispatch** to send it at once.
   - *Pending*: the phones are reserved. They cannot be sold, but they are still counted in the sending shop. You can change the
     destination or the phones, or cancel.
2. **Dispatch**: the phones become *In transit*. They leave the sending shop's stock and books and are in no shop until you confirm.
3. **Receive**: open the transfer and tick the phones that physically arrived, then **Confirm received**. They become Available in the
   receiving shop, with their cost in that shop's books.
4. **Missing phones**: anything you leave unticked makes the transfer a *Discrepancy*. Resolve it by receiving the phones later,
   **Return to sender**, or **Write off as lost** (the cost becomes a stock loss in the sending shop).
5. **Cancel**: allowed until something has arrived. The phones go back to the sending shop as available stock.

A phone is never available in two shops at once, and both shops' books balance at every step. Print the **Slip** (it names the sending
shop at the top) and send it with the phones.
