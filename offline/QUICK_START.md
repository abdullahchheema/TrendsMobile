# Quick start

## Owner (first day)
1. Sign in and change your password.
2. **Shops → All Shops**: create each shop. **Shops → Shop Accounts**: create one login per shop and give each shop its username and password.
3. **Settings → Chart of Accounts → Install** (once). Put the starting cash in with **Accounts & Finance → Money In → Money put in by the owner**.
4. Check the shop details under **Shops → Shop Accounts → Edit shop details** (name, phone, address shown on receipts).

## Shop Accountant (every day)
- **POS → New Sale**: tap a phone or scan its IMEI, type the customer's name and phone, enter the price and the money received, **Complete sale**, then print or share the receipt.
- **Purchases → New Purchase / Bulk Purchase**: write the brand, model, storage and colour (suggestions appear), scan IMEIs, enter the buying price.
- **Customers → Customer Dues**: open a name to record a payment. **Suppliers → Supplier Dues**: the same for what you owe.
- **Accounts & Finance → Expenses**: rent, electricity, salaries, repairs and so on.
- **Accounts & Finance → Cash and Bank**: balances, and moving money between your own accounts (for example a deposit).
- A return or exchange starts from the original sale in **POS → Sales History**.

## What each person sees
The owner sees every shop. A shop sees only its own stock, customers, suppliers, money and reports.
