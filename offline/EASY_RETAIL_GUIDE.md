# Simple counter guide

## Sell a phone

1. Click **New Sale** on the dashboard or sidebar.
2. Type the customer name and choose the phone from stock. Its price and PTA status fill automatically when already recorded. Choose PTA or Non-PTA if needed.
3. Enter the agreed selling price. Choose **Paid in full**, **Part payment**, or **Pay later / on credit**. Enter a bank/wallet reference for non-cash payments.
4. Click **Save sale** once. The confirmation shows the total, payment and customer balance. Print the receipt if needed.

The staff member and date are filled for convenience. Open **Staff, date, stock location and notes** to check or change them. Staff permissions still apply. A salesperson without payment permission records a credit sale; an authorized collector records the payment.

## Buy stock

1. Click **Buy Stock**. Type the supplier/seller name.
2. Choose the phone variant, quantity, PTA or Non-PTA, buying price and expected selling price. Choose phone type and condition.
3. Choose the payment arrangement and click **Save purchase & add stock**.

Stock, the supplier bill and any payment are saved together. Each phone keeps its own buying/selling price and stock reference, even for the same model/variant. No IMEI entry or IMEI scanning is required. Existing identifiers and posted history are retained privately; normal screens and receipts use stock references and PTA status. Previously unchecked phones are displayed as **Not recorded** until staff choose PTA or Non-PTA; they are never assumed compliant.

## Multiple items and reports

**POS / Multiple items** remains available for mixed phones/accessories, split payments, discounts and configured tax. Phone rows use PTA status. Reports and accounts use the same posted records from both entry screens. Exact stock references prevent selling the same phone twice.

Existing shift and cash-count controls still apply. If an old shift is open, reconcile it before recording payments. New Sale also supports shop-level payments when no shift is open.

## Demo shop

Open **Demo shop practice** from the dashboard. It selects a separate training shop with labelled sample records. New training transactions keep the demo label. Existing shops and historical accounts are not overwritten.

The starting example contains six purchases, four sales, cash/bank receipts, customer credit, supplier payments and a paid shop expense:

| Figure | PKR |
| --- | ---: |
| Purchases of stock | 500,000 |
| Sales | 295,000 |
| Cost of sold phones | 245,000 |
| Shop expense | 10,000 |
| Net profit | 40,000 |
| Customers still owe | 110,000 |
| Still owed to suppliers | 380,000 |
| Remaining stock value | 255,000 |
| Cash and bank balance | 55,000 |

The training page links directly to its sales, purchases, customer/supplier balances, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet and Trial Balance. These figures change when further training entries are posted. Run the seed command again safely: it does not duplicate the starting records.
